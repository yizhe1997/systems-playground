## Notes

- Every refusal uses the one generic body and message; the reason goes to the server log only.
- A change and its entry are one transaction; the only best-effort entries are sign-in, sign-out, refused sign-in and step-up (FR-016).
- No secret is written to a file, a log, a screenshot or an error message; `scripts/test.sh` generates fresh values for each run.
- A deviation from `screens.md` is either a defect (fix it) or a decision (ask the owner and update the Penpot frame in the same change).
- Commit after each task or small group, on the single branch `tradecraft_v2`; push nothing without the owner's say-so.
